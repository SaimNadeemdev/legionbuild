CUSTOM SUNROOM CONSTRUCTION

At Legion Construction, we specialize in designing and building beautiful, energy-efficient sunrooms that extend your living space and connect your home with the natural environment. Our custom sunrooms are built to the highest standards, providing you with a comfortable space to enjoy throughout all four seasons.

Benefits of Adding a Sunroom to Your Home

Natural light year-round

Increased living space

Enhanced property value

Connection to outdoors

Types of Sunrooms We Offer

Four-Season Sunrooms

Fully insulated and climate-controlled spaces that can be enjoyed year-round in any weather condition.

Three-Season Sunrooms

Perfect for spring, summer, and fall use with large windows and screens for comfortable enjoyment.

Solarium Sunrooms

Glass-enclosed spaces with glass roofs that provide panoramic views and maximum natural light.

Patio Enclosures

Convert existing patios into protected living spaces while maintaining an open-air feel.

Our Sunroom Construction Process

01

Design Consultation

We discuss your vision, space requirements, and preferences to create the perfect sunroom design.

02

Detailed Planning

Our team creates architectural plans, selects materials, and secures necessary permits.

03

Construction

Our skilled craftsmen build your sunroom with precision and attention to detail.

04

Final Inspection

We conduct a thorough inspection and walkthrough to ensure everything meets our high standards.

Sunroom Features and Options

Customize your sunroom with a variety of features and options:

  • Energy-efficient windows and doors
  • Heating and cooling systems
  • Custom lighting solutions
  • Ceiling fans and ventilation
  • Window treatments and blinds
  • Flooring options (tile, hardwood, etc.)
  • Electrical outlets and connectivity
  • Built-in planters and garden features
