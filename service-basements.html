PROFESSIONAL BASEMENT FINISHING

At Legion Construction, we specialize in transforming underutilized basement spaces into beautiful, functional living areas that add value to your home. Our expert team handles every aspect of your basement renovation, from initial design through final finishing touches, ensuring high-quality results that exceed your expectations.

Why Finish Your Basement?

Increased living space

Enhanced property value

Cost-effective expansion

Improved energy efficiency

Our Basement Finishing Process

01

Inspection & Planning

We assess your basement's condition, address moisture issues, and create a detailed plan.

02

Design & Material Selection

Our team works with you to design the space and select the perfect materials for your project.

03

Construction

Our skilled craftsmen transform your basement with framing, electrical, plumbing, and finishing work.

04

Final Inspection

We perform a thorough quality check and walkthrough to ensure your complete satisfaction.

Basement Finishing Features

Our comprehensive basement finishing services include:

  • Moisture and waterproofing solutions
  • Proper insulation and climate control
  • Optimized lighting design for lower ceilings
  • Electrical upgrades and outlet planning
  • Plumbing installation for bathrooms and wet bars
  • Drywall installation and finishing
  • Flooring solutions for basement environments
  • Egress solutions and safety features
