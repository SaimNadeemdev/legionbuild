OUTDOOR LIVING ENHANCEMENT

CUSTOM PERGOLA INSTALLATION

At Legion Construction, we design and build beautiful, functional pergolas that create defined outdoor living spaces while providing partial shade and architectural interest. Our custom pergolas are crafted with high-quality materials and expert craftsmanship to ensure they enhance your outdoor space for years to come.

Benefits of Adding a Pergola to Your Home

Filtered shade and sun protection

Defined outdoor living spaces

Enhanced architectural interest

Increased property value

Pergola Styles We Offer

Attached Pergolas

Connected to your home to create a seamless transition between indoor and outdoor living spaces.

Freestanding Pergolas

Independent structures that can be placed anywhere in your yard to create distinct outdoor rooms.

Louvered Pergolas

Adjustable slat systems that allow you to control the amount of sunlight and shade.

Arched Pergolas

Elegant designs featuring curved top beams for a distinctive architectural statement.

Premium Pergola Materials

We offer a variety of high-quality materials to suit your style, budget, and maintenance preferences:

Western Red Cedar

  • Natural beauty and aroma
  • Naturally resistant to decay
  • Excellent insect resistance
  • Lightweight yet strong

Pressure-Treated Wood

  • Cost-effective solution
  • Long-lasting durability
  • Can be stained or painted
  • Resistant to insects and rot

Vinyl/PVC

  • Virtually maintenance-free
  • Won't crack, peel, or fade
  • Available in various colors
  • Long-lasting performance

Aluminum

  • Extremely durable and strong
  • Resistant to rust and corrosion
  • Available in various finishes
  • Ideal for louvered systems

Pergola Customization Options

Enhance your pergola with a variety of custom features:

  • Integrated lighting systems
  • Ceiling fans for air circulation
  • Climbing plant supports
  • Retractable shade canopies
  • Rain sensors for louvered systems
  • Outdoor speakers and entertainment
  • Built-in heating elements
  • Decorative post caps and trim
