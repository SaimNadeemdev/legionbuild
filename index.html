FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

YOUR RENOVATION QUESTIONS ANSWERED

Legion Construction specializes in comprehensive residential construction services including custom kitchen and bathroom renovations, sunroom additions, basement finishing, deck and balcony construction, pergola installation, and complete home additions. We handle projects from initial design through final construction with an emphasis on quality craftsmanship.

Project timelines vary based on scope and complexity. A typical kitchen renovation might take 4-6 weeks, while a home addition could take 2-4 months. During your consultation, we'll provide a detailed timeline tailored to your specific project. We pride ourselves on clear communication and adherence to established schedules to minimize disruption to your daily life.

Our process begins with an initial consultation to understand your vision and needs. We then move to design and planning, creating detailed blueprints and material selections. After finalizing budgets and contracts, we handle all permitting before beginning construction. Throughout the build, we maintain regular communication and quality checks, concluding with a thorough walkthrough and final inspection to ensure your complete satisfaction.

Yes, Legion Construction stands behind our work with comprehensive warranties. We provide a 1-year warranty on labor and workmanship for all projects, ensuring any construction-related issues will be promptly addressed at no additional cost. Additionally, we pass along all manufacturer warranties for materials and fixtures installed during your project, which often extend beyond our workmanship guarantee.

We provide detailed, transparent cost estimates before beginning any work. Our payment schedule typically follows project milestones: an initial deposit to secure materials, followed by progress payments at key stages of completion. We work diligently to stay within budget and promptly communicate any unforeseen circumstances that might affect costs. Our goal is to eliminate financial surprises and ensure you're comfortable with every aspect of the project's financial management.
